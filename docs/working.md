# Working in the app

## Opening where you left off

The app opens the vault and the place it was showing when it was last used — the template and its tab. If that vault can no longer be opened (moved, or a share out of reach), it starts from the first screen. A vault opened for the first time shows the first template that has documents. Where the app was is kept in its settings on this device, never in the vault.

## Keyboard

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| Ctrl+S (⌘S) | Saves what is being edited — a document or a template — from any field, or with focus on the page itself |
| Ctrl+N | Starts a new document of the template shown, from any of its tabs |
| Ctrl+F | Goes to the box that finds the template's documents |

While a dialog is open these keys do nothing: the question on screen is answered first. A suggested value is a button beside its field: Tab reaches it and Enter takes it, and focus goes back to the field.

## Narrow windows

A template's documents are laid out in three columns: the sidebar, the list of documents (as wide as the sidebar), and the document picked in it. The list and the document scroll on their own. A new document puts the cursor in its first field, and saving leaves it in the field being written.

From 1024 pixels wide the toolbar button folds the sidebar to a narrow rail and back. Below that the sidebar is a drawer over the content. It starts closed; the toolbar button opens it, and picking a place, clicking outside it or Escape closes it.

## Unsaved edits

Unsaved edits are never dropped without asking: opening something else, switching views, opening another vault or closing the window asks first.
